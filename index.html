CASE STUDY 01

Reclaimed — building suspense for an interactive story

The challenge was to communicate danger, mystery and character relationships while working within the visual language of the Episode platform. I combined character assets with an original atmospheric background, then used lighting, colour grading and focal contrast to create a cinematic cover that reads clearly at mobile size.

CASE STUDY 02

Flames — making typography part of the action

This panel explores how lettering can behave like an illustrated element rather than an overlay. I drew the type directly in Procreate, matching its rhythm, edges and direction to the movement of the fire. The result connects sound, motion and image in a single visual moment.

About me

Artwork that feels true to the story it belongs to.

I’m Mabelle Louve, an illustrator and designer focused on narrative-led visual work. I create book covers, graphic novel imagery, posters and commissioned pieces that balance strong composition with emotion and atmosphere.

I work primarily in Procreate and Photoshop. I’m especially drawn to projects where character, mood and storytelling matter—and where the final artwork needs to feel like part of a larger world.

I’m open to commissions, collaborations and new creative projects.
